Our story

A recipe book that became a brand

Every Ajji Ruchi pack carries the taste of one family kitchen.

Ajji Ruchi is inspired by the recipes and cooking of the founder’s grandmother, Ajji. Those flavours came down through the family, and the family business is now stepping into homemade-style packaged products that are ready to be savoured.

We make unique, healthy South Indian masala powders with pure ingredients and recipes passed down through generations, so a busy kitchen can taste home in minutes.
